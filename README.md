## About

3.5+ years building production AI systems at L&T — from multi-agent orchestration and RAG pipelines to real-time computer vision at industrial scale. Currently an AI Engineer at L&T Energy & Hydrocarbon, leading development of agentic compliance systems and safety intelligence pipelines deployed across 6 industrial sites.

I own the full loop: architecture, implementation, evaluation, and delivery. Every system I ship has observability built in. Every RAG pipeline has retrieval benchmarks. Every agent has structured logging and tracing against ground truth.

My background spans the full stack — from YOLO-based CV pipelines and LangGraph supervisor-worker architectures to FastAPI backends and React frontends. I design things and then make those designs real through code.
